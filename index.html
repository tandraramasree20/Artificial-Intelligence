01 / COURSE OVERVIEW

Understand the ideas behind intelligent systems.

The course introduces intelligent agents, search strategies, knowledge representation, software agents, planning and natural language processing. The final unit considers generative AI, ethics, privacy and emerging trends.

Prerequisites listed in the supplied syllabus: Python and Data Structures.

07 / RESPONSIBLE AI

Use AI as a learning partner, not a substitute for understanding.

Verify. Check facts, reasoning, code and references.

Disclose. Follow faculty instructions on AI-use acknowledgement.

Protect. Never enter personal, confidential or restricted institutional data into unapproved tools.

Own your learning. Be ready to explain and defend submitted work.
